Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Ultimate Pub Quiz (“the App”) is a casual quiz game for Android and the web. This policy explains what limited data is processed when you play.

Who we are

The App is published under the package name com.pubquiznight.app. Contact: devs@legiongames.uk

Data we process

  • Solo / local play: quiz progress stays on your device (in the app WebView). We do not require an account.
  • Vs mode: team names you enter (up to 24 characters), match codes, selected categories, question decks for that match, and round scores are sent to our match server so devices can play the same quiz and compare scores.
  • Advertising (Android): the Play build shows a standard bottom banner via Google AdMob. Google may use the advertising ID and device / ad interaction data to serve and measure ads under Google’s policies.

What we do not collect

  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No sale of personal data and no use of match data for advertising profiles by the App publisher.

Ads

On Android, the App displays a standard AdMob banner (phone size 320×50 dp, or an anchored adaptive banner of equivalent height). Ad serving is handled by Google Mobile Ads. See Google’s advertising and privacy documentation for how AdMob processes data. The web / PWA build may show non-personalised in-house promo placeholders instead of AdMob.

Why we process Vs data

Match information is used only to run head-to-head games: pairing devices, sharing the same questions, and comparing scores after each round.

Retention & security

Match rooms are temporary and are automatically deleted after a few hours of inactivity. Traffic to the match server uses HTTPS. The Android app blocks cleartext (non-HTTPS) network traffic.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience. Do not enter personal information about children in team names. If you believe we have received such information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can play solo without using Vs mode. Uninstalling the App removes local WebView data on the device.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or match service changes. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.

Contact

Email devs@legiongames.uk with privacy questions.
